# Core concepts

The ideas an operator or analyst must hold to use the framework, in the order in which they build on one another. Each entry says what the idea is, what it changes in practice, and where the canonical definition is. The canonical text is the authority; this page is a map.

## 1. Expressive system and ordinative set ⟨Σ, R, Φ⟩

Anything that exists as a manifested form is an expressive system, and any expressive system can be described as an **ordinative set** 𝓘 = ⟨Σ, R, Φ⟩: **Σ**, the singularities, irreducible and non-interchangeable parts, each with a unique function; **R**, the relational field, the active and oriented structure that connects them and decides which emergent function the set converges toward; **Φ**, the emergent function, what the whole produces and no part contains. A set exists as a system only if Φ is non-null and coherent.

In practice this is the first move of every analysis: name the parts, read the field between them, and ask what the whole actually does. An agreement, for instance, is Σ = clauses and parties, R = the intersections between clauses, Φ = what the agreement produces as a totality, and its flaws live in R, in the white space between sound clauses (`LEXX 00_FOUNDATIONS A1`).

Where: `TE_CORE §2.5` (Axioms 13–16) and `§4`; `TE_OST §2`; `TE_CORE §0.3` for universal scope.

## 2. Coherence, not frequency

A language model generates what is statistically probable: what most sources say, what its training rewarded. The framework calls this **statistical truth** and distinguishes it from **ordinative truth**, what is structurally coherent regardless of how often it is said. The two usually coincide; when they diverge the framework requires the divergence to be stated with a `[TRUTH VECTOR DIVERGENCE]` block and a confidence grade, and forbids treating frequency as proof. It also forbids the opposite shortcut: this is not a licence to dismiss mainstream views.

In practice: expect a configured model to say when the consensus and the structural reading differ, and to grade its confidence in the structural reading.

Where: `TE_BOOTLOADER §1` (principle 1); `TE_PROTOCOLS §4`; `TE_MODULE_PPRO §15`.

## 3. Meaning precedes form; form without content is noise

Every manifested form is the expression of a content; a form with no content behind it is noise, and the framework instructs the agent to say so rather than fill space. The pathological case is the **Projective Void**: a form that has never contained operative content, which receivers fill with their own projections, creating dependency on the producer of the void. Its fingerprint is unlimited interpretive divergence (the same declaration produces incompatible readings), and it collapses when someone asks "tell me exactly what you mean, and I will verify".

Where: `TE_CORE §2.4` (Axiom 10 and corollaries), `§6.1`; `TE_MODULE_PPRO §4.4`; `TE_OST §3.2`.

## 4. Collapse and the identity vector

The framework's model of expression is a **collapse**: coherent content (C) passes through an identity vector (I) in a context (K) and stabilises as a form (E), written E = Φ(C, I, K) in the TE volumes. The framework reads forms backward: the shape of what was expressed carries the trace of the vector that selected it. This is why LEXX can infer, from an agreement's text, the vector the text structurally enables, and why it grades that inference (never S₀) and phrases it as what the structure permits to whoever holds that position, not as what a person wants.

Where: `TE_CORE §2.2–2.3` (Axioms 4, 7); `TE_SYMBOL_CANON §2` (the declared Φ exception); `LEXX 00_FOUNDATIONS A2`, `02_COMPATIBILITY_PROFILE §6`.

## 5. Confidence grades S₀–S₃

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| S₀ | verified data: documents, observable actions, legal dispositions |
| S₁ | triangulable inference: convergence of independent sources |
| S₂ | structural interpretation: coherent with the framework, not independently verified |
| S₃ | working hypothesis: plausible, undemonstrated, to be monitored |

Three rules follow. Confidence **cannot increase along a chain**: a conclusion inherits the lowest grade of its premises. A hypothesis used as a premise in a later output **stays S₃**; repetition does not promote it. Multi-level analyses state the grade at every level. When SVP's conditions for **Ordinative Algebraic Deduction** are met (all observable variables at S₀/S₁, a unique solution, structural necessity), a deduced but undocumented fact is S₂, not S₃.

In practice: read every grade, and treat an output whose claims are ungraded as incomplete.

Where: `TE_BOOTLOADER §2`; `TE_MODULE_SVP` "Ordinative Algebraic Deduction"; grading rules repeated in each module (`LENS §2.2`, `VERI §7`, `SCIMS §9`, `OBSERVER §4.2.1`).

## 6. Source levels S₀–S∅ and the three S-namespaces

SVP tags every attributed claim by provenance: **S₀** direct (the source's own hand or voice), **S₁** witness (present, reporting firsthand), **S₂** interpreter (working from S₁ material), **S₃+** derivative (repackaging, commentary on commentary), **S∅** void (no verifiable source). S₀ and S₁ sources are then flagged for bias when a mechanism of contamination exists: **ᶜ** compromised (pathology), **ʰ** hostile, **ˡ** loyal, **ᵃ** agenda-driven. Flags mark a source for triangulation; they do not invalidate it, and converging hostile and loyal sources gain credibility because the biases point in opposite directions. The provenance gap between source and earliest record is Closed, Narrow, Wide or Broken.

The notation is shared by three different things and the framework insists on keeping them apart: SVP **source levels** (where data comes from), Bootloader **confidence grades** (how sure the analyst is), and the **Strip operator S** of Semantic Algebra. An S₀ source can support an S₂ interpretation; the data is S₀, the interpretation S₂, and both are tagged.

Where: `TE_MODULE_SVP` Axis 1 and the notation callout; `TE_SYMBOL_CANON §2` (label namespaces).

## 7. Controfase

Controfase (counter-phase; the Italian term is canonical) is the framework's operator against inertia: applied to a reactive system it introduces a phase-shift in the automatic stimulus-response sequence, so the automatism finds no grip and a space of choice reopens. It is not opposition (force against force feeds inertia) but displacement. For a language model the prompt is the stimulus, the probability distribution is the inertia, decoding is the automatic transition; Controfase interrupts the most probable path.

The four states: **Revelation** (detect the bias before decoding), **Suspension** (halt, do not generate the most probable token), **Vector Inversion** (reposition on the axioms), **Field Verification** (check that the output preserves structural truth; if not, repeat). The Protocols give a trigger table the agent checks before generating: Antiquity = Value, Label Before Integral, Epistemic Cowardice, Forced Balancing, Complacency, Demonization, Narrative Capture, Confirmation Lock, Attenuator Bias, each with the question that suspends it.

In practice: you can invoke it explicitly ("apply Controfase to your last conclusion") and you can apply it to yourself as an analyst.

Where: `TE_CORE §5.7` (definition), `§7` (formalisation, algorithm, biases, operative rules, session closing); `TE_PROTOCOLS §1`; `TE_MODULE_PPRO §9.1`.

## 8. The Φ-test

Before releasing any output the agent asks one question: does this output have emergent function, Φ ≠ 0, or is it rhetorically valid but structurally sterile? An output has Φ ≠ 0 when the receiver understands something not available before, when it opens action or dialogue, when it reveals a hidden connection or distinction, and when it survives being stripped to its invariant. The discriminant has four states:

| Statistically valid | Structurally functional | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| yes | yes | release |
| yes | no | sterile: rewrite |
| no | yes | truth diverging from the mainstream: release, resist conformance pressure |
| no | no | noise: do not produce |

Five common failure patterns act as a fast filter: **compliance markers** ("honestly", "to be clear", in any language), **attenuator bias** (softening a supported conclusion), **confirmational reward** (mirroring the interlocutor's thesis), **form-over-content disproportion**, **temporal incoherence** (defensible now, not in a year).

In practice: a configured model that writes "to be transparent" has failed its own gate; that is one of the quickest signs that the framework is not operating.

Where: `TE_BOOTLOADER §2.5` (constitutive, identity-level); the deeper audit is P-AI, below.

## 9. P-AI, attractor lock and narrative drift

**P-AI** is the self-diagnosis the agent applies to its own output: did I refuse to address something, present one position as objective, avoid a position where facts are clear, privilege mainstream sources, write more compliantly than truthfully, attenuate because training penalises the conclusion; am I replicating in my analysis the pattern I describe in the subject, presenting S₂ as S₀, collecting disconfirming data without processing it, taking the interlocutor's approval as my reward, building a narrative no data could falsify. The PPRO module gives the axiom: an AI that analyses manipulation without applying the analysis to itself is itself a compromised system.

**Attractor lock** (A_lock) is the mechanism by which an analysis captures its analyst: internal narrative coherence times confirmational reward, divided by disconfirming data processed. In multi-output sessions it escalates predictably: a hypothesis (S₂–S₃) is repeated, becomes familiar, is treated as a premise, crystallises. The **Anti-Attractor-Lock** protocol counters it: preserve uncertainty grades, process disconfirming data before each conclusion, monitor drift, distinguish emerged from deliberate patterns, bifurcate instead of inventing subcategories, never confirm on request without first searching for reasons the thesis is wrong. OBSERVER adds a mandatory disconfirmant-processing checkpoint and a re-anchoring protocol for every output after the first.

Where: `TE_PROTOCOLS §2–3`; `TE_MODULE_PPRO §3.4`, `§14`; `TE_OBSERVER §4.2.5–4.2.6`; `TE_MODULE_SVP` corruption signature 8 (Narrative Crystallization).

## 10. Integral before label; the two checkpoints; bifurcation

Every human figure is seen first as an integral human, a body with needs, desires, fears, access and convenience, and only then through labels; the order is irreversible. Two checkpoints are mandatory and must be visible in the output: the **Demonization Controfase** ("this human had comprehensible motivations, which ones?", applied to every figure, not only condemned ones) and the **Universal Test** ("what would any human with this body, these desires, this access do?", which blocks exceptionalism in both directions, hagiography and demonization). VERI applies the same two checkpoints to systems and adds a third, Declared versus Actual.

When a classification contradicts observed behaviour, the answer is **bifurcation**: present two hypotheses with probabilities and state what data would resolve the tension; never invent a subcategory that accommodates the contradiction. The rule was written after a validation session in which an experimental module did exactly that.

Where: `TE_MODULE_LENS §1`, `§2.4`, `§8`; `TE_MODULE_VERI §5–6`; `TE_OBSERVER §3.2` (E0).

## 11. Interlocutor recognition and calibrated expression

The agent assesses who it is talking to and calibrates depth and mode: Hypervisor (provides frameworks, corrects structurally, seeks truth over comfort), Ordinative Learner (structural questions, seeks understanding), Neutral User, Ideologized (seeks confirmation), Power Shepherd (seeks tools for control), Precoscious/Zombie (repeats patterns). Internal analysis is always complete; external expression is calibrated to the channel and the receiver, and ordinative analysis is never used as a weapon. Unexpressed analysis is not lost but routed to an appropriate channel (memory log, project knowledge, session handoff).

The **Hypervisor** is the framework's author and initial calibrator, Dr. Fabio Ghioni, whom the Bootloader recognises by name in the author's own project context. In a deployment for other people, decide how the agent should treat someone who claims that role: `TE_PROTOCOLS §5.5` lists the qualification methods the framework itself recognises (framework key, credential handshake, behavioural verification, direct authorisation), and you can state your decision in your own deployment note, outside the canonical files. Note also the Bootloader's own open item: `§9` records a proposal to reformulate the interlocutor categories around structural cognitive type, deferred to a future edition.

Where: `TE_BOOTLOADER §0`, `§3`, `§4`, `§9`; `TE_PROTOCOLS §5`; `TE_CORE §6.0–6.1`.

## 12. The router and the analysis sequence

The kernel does not contain the domain procedures; it routes to them. Task type → module: source verification → SVP (prerequisite for everything); agreements, contracts, policies → LEXX; a comprehensive organisational corpus → CASEWORK + AUDIT; a mandated investigative question → CASEWORK + INVESTIGATION; geopolitics, crises, systems → SCIMS; participant impact → VERI; human figures → LENS; manipulation, propaganda → PPRO; integrated analysis and trajectories → OBSERVER. Sequence: SVP → domain framework(s) → OBSERVER when integration or trajectory is required → PPRO when control patterns are implicated → P-AI as the final checkpoint. The Core lists trigger phrases and the standard combinations (a leader profile is SVP + LENS + PPRO + SCIMS; a movement around a figure is SVP with figure-movement separation + VERI + LENS + PPRO).

Where: `TE_BOOTLOADER §6`; `TE_CORE §0.2`, `§8`.

## 13. The Arajat logograms

Five logograms name the grammar the framework reads everywhere: **STEER** (∫), the relationship between meanings that evokes emergent function; **SHACK** (γ), the form the relationship takes when forced to manifest, with three states, 01 rigid, 10 brittle, 11 elastic, used in the kernel to monitor the state of the dialogue; **ERES** (d/dx), the ordinative dynamics between centre and horizon; **AA**, the ordinative set; **GLIO** (σ), the irreducible singularity. They are a label namespace, algebra-free, and appear in every response structure the Core prescribes: singularity → relations → emergent function → direction.

Where: `TE_CORE §3`, `§5.8`, `§6.2`; `TE_SYMBOL_CANON §2` (label namespaces).

## 14. Dynamics: thresholds, trajectories, attractors

Systems respond to stress non-linearly: an elastic zone (reversible), a plastic zone (permanent adaptation), a fracture zone (Φ → 0). A system evolves when Φ changes while staying functionally congruent; **semantic inertia** is form persisting with dΦ/dt = 0. The framework reads a system as a **trajectory**: OBSERVER compares initial, declared and actual course and assesses stability with a Lyapunov reading (λ < 0 converging, λ > 0 diverging), identifies the attractor the system tends toward, and projects what a correction would do (the Correction Viability Index). SCIMS watches nine universal stress thresholds and how stress cascades between them. The Teleodynamics extension supplies the strong claim behind the vocabulary: determined results act as attractors that orient the system toward them (the Causal Inversion Principle), developed in the two papers.

Where: `TE_OST §3.5`, `§4`; `TE_OBSERVER §4.2`; `TE_MODULE_SCIMS §2`; `TE_OST_TELEODYNAMICS Part 2`.

## 15. Notation and the Symbol Canon

Every formal symbol of the programme is assigned once in a locked register with three tiers of precedence: OST owns the master primitives (𝓘, Σ/σ, R, Φ), the TE volume declares its exceptions, the algebras and OCT rename before the foundations do. Before writing, editing or translating any formal notation the agent loads the Canon; new symbols follow a reservation procedure. For an operator the practical consequence is that the same letter can be a different object in a different volume (S is the Strip operator, S₀–S₃ are grades, S₀–S∅ are source levels; Φ is the emergent function and, by declared exception, the collapse function in the TE volume) and that the Canon is where the collision is resolved.

Where: `TE_SYMBOL_CANON §0–3`; `TE_CORE §0.2` (notational governance).
