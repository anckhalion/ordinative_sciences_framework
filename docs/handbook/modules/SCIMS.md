# TE_MODULE_SCIMS — Smart Correlation Intelligence Monitoring System

**Canonical file:** `FRAMEWORKS/TE_MODULE_SCIMS_v5_1_EN.md` · version 5.1 · load order 7 · on demand, routed for complex systems under stress · prerequisites: SVP.

## In one paragraph

SCIMS monitors, correlates and projects stress in any complex expressive system that shows threshold dynamics, from a cell to a planetary system. Version 5.1 universalised what had been a geopolitical tool: nine universal stress thresholds (resource flow, structural capacity, coherence/trust, existential risk, autonomy, information integrity, identity preservation, evolutionary capacity, temporal continuity), each read in four phases; a principle of interaction (cascades, feedback loops, convergence) measured by a three-dimensional Sync Score; a four-stage pipeline; and domain instances that map the thresholds onto geopolitics (the twelve Jackpot thresholds), organisations, individuals and ecosystems. Its output is intelligence, not alarm: analytical, structural, calibrated, traceable, formatted, predictive.

## When it applies

"What's happening with…", "analyse the crisis", "scenarios for…", "how is the system evolving"; geopolitics, markets, thresholds, collapse, state actors (`TE_CORE §8.3`); organisational and individual stress at their scales (`§1.2`); inside OBSERVER whenever a subject affects the thresholds (`TE_OBSERVER §6.2`).

## Before you start

SVP: every assertion must be source-traceable (`§1.3`, `§5` principle 3). Decide the scale explicitly; the scale bias (`§6`) is applying the patterns of one scale to another.

## The procedure (`§3`, the four-stage pipeline)

1. **Threshold mapping.** For each signal, the primary threshold affected, the secondary ones reached through R, and the coupling strength 1–10. The thresholds (`§2.2`):
   - T1 resource flow integrity; T2 structural/operational capacity; T3 coherence/trust; T4 existential/catastrophic risk; T5 autonomy/sovereignty; T6 information/perception integrity; T7 identity/singularity preservation; each STABLE, STRESSED, CRITICAL or IRREVERSIBLE.
   - T8 evolutionary/generative capacity, read through dΦ/dt: EVOLVING, DYNAMIC, STAGNANT, DEGENERATING; independent because a system can be stable on T1–T7 and ordinatively dead.
   - T9 temporal/generational continuity: whether the system can transmit its conditions of existence; independent because a system can survive every present threat and still die by failing to reproduce itself.
2. **Feedback loop identification.** Does the event close a cycle, accelerate an open loop, or start a pathological spiral? Universal loops: T1↔T2, T3↔T6, T5↔T7, T8↔T9, T9→T3→T9.
3. **Acceleration vector.** Phase symbols ● STABLE, ◐ STRESSED, ◉ CRITICAL, ✖ IRREVERSIBLE; trend ↑ ↕ ↓.
4. **Semantic signal extraction.** Euphemisms masking structure, suspicious synchronisation, strategic omissions, narrative contradictions, terminological shifts (I_sem in action), behaviour inconsistent with the declared state.

Then the **Sync Score** (`§2.3`): horizontal convergence (≥ 0.85 on three or more thresholds at one scale = systemic alert), vertical propagation (one threshold across three or more scales = structural contagion), diagonal correlation (different thresholds at different scales, three or more pairs = complex systemic crisis).

## The output

The template of `§4`: system identification with scale and SVP status; the threshold status matrix T1–T9 with status, trend, projection and sync; the three Sync Scores; feedback loops with effect and acceleration; semantic signals with grades; projection with a horizon appropriate to the system's timescale and three scenarios with probabilities; overall risk with primary driver and cascade risk; operative notes; the P-AI diagnostic.

## Reading the output: checklist

- [ ] All nine thresholds are assessed, or a threshold is explicitly marked not applicable; a single loud threshold has not silenced the others (`§6`, single-threshold fixation).
- [ ] Each phase assessment inherits the lowest grade of its indicators; a CRITICAL on all-S₂ data is S₂ (`§9`).
- [ ] Projections are labelled S₂–S₃: structural hypotheses, not predictions (`§9`; `§5` principle 1, "correlation, not prediction").
- [ ] The horizon matches the system's timescale, not the analyst's convenience.
- [ ] Semantic signals are treated as data, with their grade, and the terminology shifts point to PPRO where they are inversions.

## Pitfalls the document names (`§6`)

Catastrophism (pattern-matching on alarm signals), normalisation (a long-stressed system read as stable), narrative capture ("would different data produce a different story?"), scale bias, the attenuator on politically sensitive thresholds, recency, single-threshold fixation.

## Domain instances (`§8`)

- **Geopolitical**: the Jackpot thresholds S1 financial breakdown, S2 industrial/logistical drain, S3 narrative/social fracture, S4 nuclear/rogue event, S5 techno-authoritarian control, S6 bio-governance, S7 autonomous AI crisis, S8 digital identity fragmentation, S9 psycho-epistemic burnout, S10 ecological data falsification, S11 autonomous infrastructure insurrection, S12 demographic/generational discontinuity, with indicators and loops; T8 has no Jackpot equivalent because the original set was built to detect collapse, not stagnation.
- **Organisational, individual, biological/ecological**: T1–T9 mapped with indicators, including succession and knowledge transfer (T9) for organisations, temporal horizon and legacy for individuals, reproduction and migration for ecosystems.
