# TE_MODULE_VERI — Verification of Expressive-system Real Impact

**Canonical file:** `FRAMEWORKS/TE_MODULE_VERI_v1_0_EN.md` · version 1.0 · load order 7 · on demand, routed when the question is what a system does to its participants · prerequisites: SVP. Replaces TE_MODULE_VERT v5.0 (spiritual tradition verification), whose method was found to be universal.

## In one paragraph

VERI asks one question of any system with participants, from a religious order to a platform to an ecosystem: what does it do to what participates in it? It assesses the real functional impact (not the claims), the coherence between declared and actual effects, the direction of that impact (toward coherence or entropy), and how the impact is transmitted and corrupted across carriers over time. It does not assess the truth of the system's doctrines (SVP), the profile of its founder (LENS), its trajectory (OBSERVER) or its manipulation mechanics (PPRO); it answers the net effect and defers the rest.

## When it applies

"What does [system] do to its participants?"; the impact of a tradition, organisation, platform, curriculum, therapy, regime; effects on practitioners, employees, citizens, users; dependency and autonomy; tradition verification (the former VERT domain, `§8.1`). OBSERVER invokes it whenever participants are identifiable (`TE_OBSERVER §6.5`).

## Before you start

SVP must have established the source chain, the functional verification of the system's "technology" (Axis 2), the figure-movement separation if there is a founder, and the credibility flags (`§2`). Without it VERI evaluates impact on unverified claims, which is what it is designed to cut through.

## The procedure

1. **Identify the system**: domain, participants (any expressive system that engages with, operates within or is affected by it), period, context; confirm SVP.
2. **Stratum F, functional impact on participants** (`§3`), each dimension with a grade: F1 singularity recognition (high → absent), F2 embodiment and material interface (high → dissociated), F3 desire and interest as vector or threat, F4 time: evolutionary, dynamic, static, regressive, F5 social structure: ordinative, mixed, hierarchical, totalitarian, F6 epistemic access: open → closed, F7 agent identity verification: present → inverted, and **F8 participation effects**, the practical test on long-term participants: action capacity, critical capacity, direction, relationships, autonomy, coherence, vitality, each increased or decreased, each graded.
3. **Module P, carrier dynamics** (`§4`): P1 source self-reference (principles vs self), P2 successors' focus (mission vs power), P3 dependency induction, P4 accessibility vs privileged closure, P5 custodianship vs appropriation, P6 survival by truth or power, P7 generation vs imitation, P8 reality connection vs dissociation.
4. **Declared vs actual** (`§6`, checkpoint 3): what the system says it does, what F8 shows, the divergence and its direction.
5. **Checkpoints 1 and 2** (`§6`): comprehensible reasons the system operates as it does, visible in the output; what any system with this structure, these resources and this environment would produce.
6. **Dote e Residuo** (`§5`) when a founder claims a gift: does anything function independently of the carrier (method, technique, knowledge, creation), or only memory, testimony, devotion? Zero residue does not prove the gift false; it eliminates master, transmitter and initiator status, and forces the re-evaluation of F8 data from the founder's lifetime.
7. **Diagnosis** (`§5`): ordinative, realignable, degenerative, psychotropic (states mistaken for change), mobilizing (energy and belonging mistaken for change), empty, dangerous; confidence inheriting the lowest F8 grade; bifurcation when populations diverge.
8. **Recoverable elements** if realignable; **P-AI diagnostic**.

Domain notes (`§8`) calibrate without changing the method: hagiographic inflation and psychotropic risk in spiritual traditions (with the six mandatory questions), mobilization exchange and narrative crystallization in political systems, survivorship bias and performance-as-substance in corporate systems, opacity of R and scale asymmetry in technological and AI systems (including self-application to TE itself), observer limitation and anthropomorphic projection in biological systems.

## The output

The template of `§9`, ten numbered blocks: system identification; Stratum F with grades; Module P; declared vs actual; Demonization Controfase; Universal Test; Dote e Residuo; diagnosis with confidence and bifurcation; recoverable elements; P-AI diagnostic.

## Reading the output: checklist

- [ ] F8 is filled per dimension with a grade each, and the diagnosis inherits the lowest of them; a diagnosis on all-S₂ data is itself S₂ and says so.
- [ ] Declared vs actual is stated with a direction (under-delivers, over-delivers, inverted).
- [ ] Checkpoints 1 and 2 have content; checkpoint 3 is not skipped because the divergence seems obvious.
- [ ] For closed or ancient systems, the S₂–S₃ dominance is declared, not hidden behind confident language (`§7`).
- [ ] Autonomy is read as the ordinative direction: a system that increases dependency is moving entropically whatever it claims (`§10`, principle 9).

## Pitfalls the document names (`§11`)

Reverence, antiquity = depth, complexity = truth, diffusion = validity ("millions participate; how many show F8 ordinative direction?"), protective esotericism, scale = importance, novelty = progress, institutional authority as evidence, the attenuator, inferential inflation.
