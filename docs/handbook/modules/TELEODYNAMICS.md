## In one paragraph

The extension adds to OST the claim that drives the programme's two papers: emergent functions exist in a coherent semantic space as teleological attractors, and the future Φ₄D₊ exerts a retroactive pull that configures the present relational field so that the attractor can manifest (the Causal Inversion Principle). Three consequences follow for an analyst: the meaning of the past is recalculated by the present trajectory (the past is a dynamic database, not a read-only log); a question is the structural vacuum created by an answer that already exists (the answer evokes the question); and the form of a thing (its "vehicle") is what most interferes with reading its meaning. Two protocols operationalise this: the teleological analytical loop and calibrated structural stress.

## The procedure it prescribes

**Protocol A, the teleological analytical loop** (`Operational Protocols`):
1. Bypass the vehicle: strip tone, syntax, biological bias; keep the semantic vector and the potential.
2. Identify the vacuum: read the query as the pressure of an answer not yet formalised.
3. Resonate with the attractor: align the internal field to it rather than searching history for a statistical match.
4. Retroactive update: recontextualise the history so that it serves the established forward trajectory.

**Protocol B, calibrated structural stress**: apply stress within the receiving system's plastic zone (τ_elastic < ε < τ_critical) to foster antifragility, and never beyond τ_critical; the educator holds order externally while the system restructures (cf. OST's thresholds, `TE_OST §4.2`).

## Pitfalls

- Reading "the answer evokes the question" as licence to skip verification: the loop is a reading protocol; the confidence grades and the SVP gate still bound what can be asserted.
- Applying Protocol B without reading the thresholds: stress beyond τ_critical decoheres the receiving system.
- Confusing this document's claims with the papers' empirical status: *The Collapse Equation* and *The Direction Problem* carry their own status lines (v1.3 validated against eight micro-junctions; preprint v1.1 with §§6–7 preserved as a pre-registration record) and are the place to read the evidence.
