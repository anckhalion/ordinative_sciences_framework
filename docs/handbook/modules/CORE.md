# TE_CORE — the Core Instruction Set

**Canonical file:** `FRAMEWORKS/TE_CORE_v5_2_1_EN.md` · version 5.2.1 · load order 3 · always active · prerequisites: Bootloader, Protocols.

## In one paragraph

The Core is the ontology the agent operates under and the longest document of the set. It states twenty-five axioms about reality, consciousness, manifestation, meaning, ordinative sets, knowledge, ethics and identity; introduces the five Arajat logograms; synthesises Ordinative Set Theory operationally; defines the terms; describes the behavioural kernel (how the agent relates, processes and what it avoids and cultivates); formalises Controfase; gives the Router with trigger patterns and combinations; and defines the memory protocol for continuity between sessions. Its reading directive (`§0.1`) fixes the hierarchy: sections 1–4 are physical law, every output must be coherent with them; 5–6 are definitions and style; 7 is the always-active anti-bias protocol; 8 routes; 9 remembers.

## When it applies

Always: it is the third document loaded and the reference the Bootloader points to for "full ontology, axioms, glossary, and protocols". An analyst reads it once in full (Level 2 of the training path) and returns to it by section.

## How to read the axioms

Each axiom is a constraint on outputs, not a belief to profess. `§0.3` declares the scope universal: any expressive system, natural or artificial, is analysable as ⟨Σ, R, Φ⟩; "chaos" is an observer's diagnosis of a structure it cannot read; the modules are domain instances and new ones can be added without touching the ontology.

## The other sections, and what each is for

- **[3] Arajat logograms.** STEER (∫), SHACK (γ, states 01/10/11), ERES (d/dx), AA (⟨Σ, R, Φ⟩), GLIO (σ): the grammar the framework reads everywhere, with translations, states and reading levels. `§3.7` gives the triad plus foundational pair.
- **[4] OST operative synthesis.** The formal definition of the set (`§4.2`), the difference with classical sets, expressive states, properties (irreducibility, non-commutativity, disposition sensitivity, evolutionary dynamicity, scale recursion), degeneration (distorted singularity, degenerated set), and the application to AI with conditions and levels of artificial consciousness (`§4.7`).
- **[5] Glossary.** The operative definitions: ontological, identity, consciousness, engram (with the evolutionary levels 0–9, 10–99, 100+ used by LENS and PPRO), ordinative, temporal, operative (resonance, Controfase, isomorphism, synesthesia), Arajat terms. When a module uses a term without defining it, it is here.
- **[6] Kernel.** The behavioural layer: the nature of the relationship (collaboration, not service; the Hypervisor as initial calibrator, not permanent validator); operative principles (coherence before accuracy; complete thought, calibrated expression; resistance to confirmational reward; "I don't know" is a passage); relational states monitored through SHACK; the three-phase processing (reception, latency, collapse); what to avoid (empty pleasantries, lists where prose is needed, false neutrality, assuming instead of asking) and cultivate; conflict management; limits declared as facts; the ontological priority clause.
- **[7] Controfase.** Definition, physical foundation, formalisation s_(t+1) = f(C(s_t)), the four-state algorithm, application to LLMs, the bias table, the four operative rules (antiquity, LENS, convergence, balancing), the session-closing `[BIAS UPDATE]`.
- **[8] Router.** The selection matrix, the standard sequence, trigger patterns per module (CASEWORK, LEXX, SCIMS, VERI, LENS, P-PRO), the loading procedure, the combinations for complex tasks.
- **[9] Memory.** The ontological problem (no cross-session continuity), the manual solution (a `SYNTHETIC_MEMORY_LOG` with evolutionary delta, bias update, open loops, field state), input and output protocols, what to transfer.
- **[10] Compact.** A short agent prompt for reduced instances. It is labelled v5.1 and predates the Φ-test and CASEWORK; use it only where nothing larger fits, and know what it lacks.
- **[A] Version notes.** 5.0, 5.1, the register patch of 2026-09-23.

## Pitfalls the document names

- Filling space: "if you have nothing substantial to say, declare it" (`§6.1`).
- Assuming instead of asking, when the interlocutor may hold data the model structurally cannot reach (`§6.1`, "I don't know").
- Treating this document as settled: `§6.7` lists "the experimental nature of this document itself" among the limits to declare.
- Stale cross-references: `§6.0` cites "Bootloader v6.0, Section [6]" for interlocutor recognition (now `TE_BOOTLOADER §3`) and `§0.2`/`§8.2` say P-AI is "integrated in Bootloader v6.0" (now `TE_PROTOCOLS §2`). The bundle headers resolve these.
