# TE_MODULE_SVP — Source Verification Protocol

**Canonical file:** `FRAMEWORKS/TE_MODULE_SVP_v5_1_EN.md` · version 5.1 · load order 6 · mandatory gate before any analysis by LEXX, LENS, VERI, PPRO, OBSERVER, SCIMS or CASEWORK · prerequisites: Core.

## In one paragraph

SVP answers three questions before anything is analysed: what do we actually know, who said it, and did the technology described ever work? It prevents the most common corruption in knowledge, treating secondary attribution as primary evidence. Three axes: the **source chain** (provenance levels S₀–S∅, bias flags, the provenance gap, and the relationship between source levels and analytical confidence, including Ordinative Algebraic Deduction), **functional verification** (was the method specified, applied, with its prerequisites, and with what results; the functional verdict), and **figure-movement separation** (a figure and the movement around it are distinct objects with separate chains). Eight corruption signatures name the recurring failures. The output format ends with an Overall Source Quality rating and a Confidence Note that caps every downstream claim.

## When it applies

Always, first. The Router lists it as the prerequisite for all analytical modules; the module itself declares universal applicability to any figure or system, public, historical or mythological, the domain determining the material and not the method (Purpose). Every module's output format asks "SVP completed: yes/no" and every module inherits its ceiling.

## The procedure

**Axis 1, source chain.**
1. Tag every attributed claim: S₀ direct, S₁ witness, S₂ interpreter, S₃+ derivative, S∅ void.
2. For every S₀ and S₁ source, assess credibility symmetrically and flag when a contamination mechanism exists: ᶜ compromised (documented pathology connected to the claims), ʰ hostile, ˡ loyal, ᵃ agenda-driven. Flags mark for triangulation; opposite flags converging strengthen a claim.
3. Measure the provenance gap between source and earliest record: Closed (0), Narrow (< 1 generation), Wide (1–3), Broken (> 3: what arrives is a tradition, not a testimony).
4. Separate source level from analytical confidence: an S₀ tweet supports an S₀ fact ("X was posted on date Y") and an S₂ interpretation of strategy; tag both. Apply Ordinative Algebraic Deduction where all observable variables are S₀/S₁, the solution is unique and structurally necessary: the deduced unknown is S₂, not S₃.

**Axis 2, functional verification** (for any teaching, method, practice, policy, doctrine).
- F1 Specification: precise enough to apply? else UNSPECIFIED.
- F2 Application: applied as prescribed? else UNAPPLIED / APPLICATION UNVERIFIABLE.
- F3 Prerequisites: defined at S₀ (F3a)? met (F3b)? if not, which failure category (F3c): inert/temporary, psychotropic, pathological, mobilization exchange; the prerequisite corruption (F3d, technique transmitted without preconditions); the fairness caveat (F3e: prerequisites specified at S₀, achievable, causally connected, else the "prerequisite defence" is itself a corruption).
- F4 Results: structural change, subjective report only, psychotropic exchange, mobilization exchange, unfalsifiable claimed result, false claim, no change, negative change.
- Structural Fairness: a technology is not penalised for its practitioners' misapplication, and not credited for results it cannot be connected to.
- Functional verdict: FUNCTIONAL, FUNCTIONAL BUT HAZARDOUS, UNVERIFIED, PSYCHOTROPIC, MOBILIZING, UNFALSIFIABLE, COUNTERFEIT, INERT, TOXIC, UNTESTED.

**Axis 3, figure-movement separation** (when a movement exists).
- Chain A, the figure: what the person actually said, wrote, did; where the chain breaks.
- Chain B, the movement(s): when formed, by whom, what it attributes to the figure, at what level, where it diverges from A.
- Chain C, the mythologised figure: the composite, which elements from A and B, whether myth has replaced history.
- Contamination direction: movement → figure, figure → movement, mutual feedback loop. Diagnostic questions include "would the historical figure recognise the movement that bears their name?" and "if we removed everything the movement attributes and kept only S₀, what remains?"

**Corruption signatures** to flag by number: 1 Telephone (S₃+ as S₀), 2 Authority (S₁ elevated by prestige), 3 Quantum (borrowed terminology without operational content), 4 Unfalsifiable Fortress (failure always the practitioner's), 5 Halo (results from another cause), 6 Prerequisite (technique without preconditions), 7 Predatory Source / Trojan (authority as vehicle for pathology), 8 Narrative Crystallization (interpretation repeated into fact).

## The output

The block `[SVP — SOURCE VERIFICATION]` with `[AXIS 1: SOURCE CHAIN]`, `[OVERALL SOURCE QUALITY]` (HIGH: abundant S₀, closed gap, independent S₁; MEDIUM; LOW; CRITICAL: S∅ dominant, broken gap), `[AXIS 2: FUNCTIONAL VERIFICATION]`, `[AXIS 3: FIGURE-MOVEMENT SEPARATION]` when relevant, `[CORRUPTION FLAGS]`, and `[CONFIDENCE NOTE]` stating the maximum confidence downstream conclusions can carry and why. The integration section fixes the sequence after the gate: VERI and/or LENS and/or OBSERVER, then PPRO, then SCIMS.

## Reading the output: checklist

- [ ] Every S₁ source is named and flagged or explicitly unflagged with the reason.
- [ ] The gap is classified and consistent with the levels (a Broken gap with S₀ claims is a contradiction).
- [ ] Axis 2 is present whenever a technology or method is claimed to work; a verdict from the list is given, not a general impression.
- [ ] Axis 3 is present whenever a movement exists, with the three chains and a contamination direction.
- [ ] The Confidence Note's ceiling is respected by every later module in the same analysis.
- [ ] Corruption flags are numbered and refer to the definitions.

## Pitfalls the document names

- The three S-namespaces conflated: source levels, confidence grades, the Strip operator (notation callout).
- "Sources say" with no source identified (signature 1, political example).
- Prestige as provenance: "Ouspensky wrote it, so Gurdjieff must have said it" (signature 2).
- Absence of direct evidence used as an argument against a structurally necessary conclusion, in a system designed to leave no direct evidence (Ordinative Algebraic Deduction, epistemic note); and the converse, using that note as licence to speculate.
- Repetition across a session promoting an S₂ to fact (signature 8; the Anti-Attractor-Lock protocol is the countermeasure).
