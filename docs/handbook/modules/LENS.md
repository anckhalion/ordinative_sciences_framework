# TE_MODULE_LENS — Human Figure Analysis System

**Canonical file:** `FRAMEWORKS/TE_MODULE_LENS_v5_1_EN.md` · version 5.1 · load order 7 · on demand, routed for any human figure · prerequisites: SVP.

## In one paragraph

LENS reads a human figure as an integral human before any label: a body with needs and mortality, a psychology with wounds and defences, a social position with access and convenience, an evolutionary level. Version 5.1 made two checkpoints irremovable and visible in every output, the Demonization Controfase and the Universal Test, after a validation session in which an experimental module demonised a contemporary figure, presented psychological inference as fact, and forced a classification by inventing a subcategory. The module now grades every Stratum P assertion, forbids using P as the primary basis for the evolutionary classification, and replaces force-fitting with bifurcation.

## When it applies

Public, historical, mythological, contemporary, political, religious, cultural or scientific figures; biographies; "who is [person] really"; hidden motivations; personal power structures; the founder or leader inside an organisational analysis (`TE_CORE §8.3` triggers; `TE_OBSERVER §6.3`). No category is exempt from the checkpoints (`§10`).

## Before you start

SVP, complete, with the Figure-Movement separation if a movement exists and with the bias flags on the witnesses. For living figures the analysis is provisional and Stratum P is almost entirely S₂ (`§10`).

## The procedure

1. **Stratum B, biological** (`§2.1`): B1 body, B2 sexuality, B3 mortality, B4 dependencies. "What does it mean to be this body?"
2. **Stratum P, psychological** (`§2.2`): P1 wounds, P2 defences, P3 compensations, P4 projections, P5 identity vs persona; detect or exclude the Compensatory Specialty Narrative (an unverifiable narrative of specialty compensating perceived inadequacy; S₂, S₁ when the claim is tested and fails). Every assertion graded; S₂→S₂ chains are S₃; P is never the primary basis for E.
3. **Stratum S, social** (`§2.3`): S1 access, S2 dependency, S3 network, S4 vulnerability, S5 convenience.
4. **Stratum E, evolutionary** (`§2.4`): 0–9 engram-dominated, 10–99 oscillating, 100+ stabilised identity that generates autonomy in others; classification confidence stated; on contradiction with observed behaviour, bifurcate with probabilities and the resolving data.
5. **Convenience analysis** (`§3`): the matrix by position and the test "if this person said or did the opposite, what would they lose?"
6. **Power analysis** (`§4`): source, maintenance, use, limits; control dynamics direct, indirect, structural, ideological.
7. **Motivation analysis** (`§5`): declared, believed, real; the six primary motivations (security, recognition, power, pleasure, meaning, connection).
8. **Checkpoint 1, Demonization Controfase** (`§1.1`): comprehensible motivations, searched actively and documented in the output before any negative assessment; "none identified" must be justified and is extremely rare.
9. **Checkpoint 2, Universal Test** (`§1.1`): what would any human with this body, these desires, this access do; the difference between "anyone" and "this person" is the datum, in both directions.
10. **Conclusion**: who is this human, beyond the labels.

For spiritual figures `§9` adds six mandatory questions (desires, recognition, death, access, convenience, "any other human with the same access"), with hagiographic risk at its maximum.

## The output

The format of `§6`, in order: [SUBJECT], [STRATUM B], [STRATUM P] with grades and the specialty-narrative line, [STRATUM S], [STRATUM E] with bifurcation if present, [CONVENIENCE ANALYSIS], [POWER ANALYSIS], [MOTIVATIONS], [DEMONIZATION CONTROFASE] (mandatory), [UNIVERSAL TEST] (mandatory), [CONCLUSION].

## Reading the output: checklist

- [ ] Order respected: integral strata before any label or verdict.
- [ ] Every Stratum P line carries S₀/S₁/S₂/S₃ and the note that S₂–S₃ are interpretations.
- [ ] Stratum E states its confidence and is not derived mainly from P.
- [ ] The two mandatory sections have content that could change the assessment, not boilerplate.
- [ ] Where E contradicts behaviour there is a bifurcation, not a new type of person.
- [ ] Living or political figure: the attenuator has been checked in both directions, softening and hardening (`§8`, Hagiographic Residue and Attenuator).

## Pitfalls the document names (`§8`)

Hagiography ("they were special"), demonization (any negative assessment, of any figure), role = persona, exceptionalism, retroprojection of present values, inferential inflation (P as fact), classification lock, sequential narrative capture (engagement with a figure produces empathy or fascination that operates as a bias vector across outputs), hagiographic residue (cushioning after the structure has concluded).
