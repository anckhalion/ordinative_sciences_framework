# Bibliography

This bibliography consolidates approximately 220 unique sources across 135 endnotes in 32 chapters. Works of antiquity and early-modern classics are listed by author with canonical title; modern works include full publication details. Works cited by abbreviation in the endnotes resolve here in full.
