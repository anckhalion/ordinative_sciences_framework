## [1] Tier precedence and resolution principles

```
OST (Tier-0 — 𝓘 = ⟨Σ, R, Φ⟩)  →  TE Vol 1 (Tier-1 — E = Φ(C, I, K))  →  PA, SA, OCT (Tier-2)
```

- **P1** OST owns the master primitives; TE Vol 1's published hygiene is respected — its deviations are *declared exceptions* with footnote (the Φ exception), never silent overloads.
- **P2** Declared coexistence by operand distinction is legitimate (precedent: TE's own Ω(I) vs Ω(S,t); κ pairwise vs 𝓚 vectors).
- **P3** Disambiguation devices, in order: subscripts → scripts/letter variants → adjacent letters. Arguments disambiguate where signatures differ.
- **P4** Tier-2 renames before Tier-0/1 does. OST-internal fixes are deferred to the OST 2nd edition batch (§4).
- **P5** Enum/classification labels (PA-OCT A/B/C/D states; SHACK 01/10/11; S₀–S₃ grades) are a label namespace — they do not collide with operators.
- **P6** The I-rule: no bare-glyph reuse across volumes for unrelated objects that co-occur in cross-volume contexts.

## [3] RESERVATION PROCEDURE — minting new notation (MANDATORY for new works)

New volumes (Controfase Treatise, Unified Spacetime/OGT, Ordinative Game Theory, OST 2nd ed, future papers) WILL need new symbols. Before introducing ANY new formal symbol:

1. **Check this register** (§2) and the historical record (`ORDINATIVE_ALGEBRAS/SYMBOL_CANON_..._DRAFT.md`) — is the glyph free at programme level? Bare glyphs that look free may be chapter-local elsewhere (γ) or logogram-reserved.
2. **Prefer**: a free glyph family > a subscripted form of a related canonical symbol > overloading (P2 coexistence only with explicit operand distinction, declared in the text).
3. **Propose to the Hypervisor** with: glyph, meaning, type, volume, collision check result.
4. Upon ratification: **add the row to THIS file**, bump the version (v1.0 → v1.1 …), note it in §6, and propagate to derived copies.
5. Never introduce a symbol "locally, to fix later" — chapter-local scoping is legitimate ONLY if declared in the volume's own notational-hygiene apparatus (TE App I model).

Known glyphs currently FREE for new works (verified 2026-06-11; 𝕊 removed 2026-06 — Controfase State Space; ϱ, 𝔸, 𝕋 removed 2026-08-19 — Lyapunov batch): 𝕁, ϰ, ζ, ξ (lowercase), 𝔊 (partially — 𝔊_0 used locally in TE ch31), most blackboard capitals except 𝕀/𝕄/𝔻/𝔽/𝕊/𝔸/𝕋, most Fraktur except 𝔉/𝔖(reserved fallback)/ℭ/𝔠(Controfase operator). Re-verify at minting time.
