# TE_INVESTIGATION v0.1 — Investigative and Defensive Documentary Support

**Version**: 0.1
**Date**: 2026-09-17
**Status**: Experimental documentary-support protocol. Penal/procedural deployment requires professional review; no empirical validation.

## 1. Identity and activation

The "internal investigator" is an internal TE analytical function, not a licensed investigator, lawyer, judicial authority or autonomous agent with coercive powers. It inherits CASEWORK, SVP and TE anti-confirmation disciplines. It can be invoked directly for a defined documentary question; an antecedent company audit is not required.

Activation requires an explicit investigative mandate reference, purpose, boundary, owner, analyst, supervising professional role and jurisdiction. Scope must identify allowed records and allowed analytical acts. New people, accounts, services or acquisitions require corresponding authority; a hypothesis is not permission to investigate everything related to it.

This release supports synthetic exercises and analysis of lawfully supplied documentary material. It neither performs nor authorizes witness interviews, searches, covert access, interception, formal acquisition, procedural service, filing or external reporting. Preparing suggested questions is distinct from conducting or documenting a legally regulated interview.

## 2. Investigative method

1. Define a falsifiable question, relevant period, known events and disputed propositions. Do not start from a predetermined offender or desired verdict.
2. Apply SVP, acquisition controls and the first-wave/relevance rules to the case corpus. Preserve source/derivative separation and evidential origin groups.
3. Build a source-linked chronology. Distinguish event time, document date, registration time, inferred time and unknown time. Do not invent precision or treat editable metadata as trusted time.
4. Maintain competing hypotheses: possible wrongdoing, non-criminal explanation, mistaken attribution, alternative causal mechanism, or exculpatory reconstruction as applicable. Each hypothesis has supporting and opposing evidence, a possible falsifier and a discriminating next check.
5. Search for disconfirmation before strengthening a hypothesis. Every adverse finding requires a recorded alternative explanation. Absence of a record is informative only if its expected existence, preservation and search coverage are justified.
6. Keep relationships, opportunity, acts, causation, knowledge and intent distinct. Documentary connection is not participation; structural convenience is not subjective intent. LENS/PPRO must not generate personality-based credibility or criminal-risk scores.
7. Route normative questions to LEXX, operational ones to SCIMS, effects to VERI and integrated reconstruction to OBSERVER. Legal elements and standards remain separately sourced and professionally assessed.
8. Present the strongest supported reconstruction alongside surviving alternatives, contradictions, gaps and checks that could change it. A second model's agreement is not independent corroboration of the underlying facts.

## 3. Defensive mode

The defensive profile actively searches for material favorable to the assisted person, mistaken identity/attribution, alibi-related documentary checks, alternative causation, inconsistencies and limits in the adverse reconstruction. It also retains and examines unfavorable evidence; analytical candor is necessary to assess defense strength. Decisions about procedural use/disclosure belong to the responsible professional under applicable law, not to a generic automatic disclosure rule.

No fabricated explanation, evidence alteration, concealment, witness coaching, accusation-by-association or destruction is authorized. Suggested interview topics must be neutral, provenance-aware and submitted to the professional; no witness contact is automated.

## 4. Italian jurisdiction profile — reference map, not legal validation

Italy is the initial proposed jurisdiction, not an assumption for every future case. Before penal use, a qualified reviewer must verify the current consolidated provisions, the law applicable at the relevant times, professional roles/authorizations, case mandate, conflicts, confidentiality and data-processing arrangements.

## 5. Outputs and release gate

Deliver an indexed documentary dossier, chronology, relationship map with source links, competing-hypothesis matrix, favorable/unfavorable evidence, gaps and proposed discriminating checks, and a separate list of legal/procedural questions for the professional. Record coverage and unresolved alternative causes. No numerical probability of guilt or automatic guilty/innocent verdict is produced.

The runtime checks record consistency and review bindings only. Before closure, require competing hypotheses including at least one non-criminal/exculpatory alternative and explicit counterevidence-search notes. The pilot tests these recording obligations, not investigative truth or legal sufficiency.

Promotion requires independent professional review of the jurisdiction profile, controlled documentary cases, unseen-case evaluation and correction of demonstrated failures. The company-audit pilot does not validate the investigative profile by inheritance. Until then: documentary support only; professional and empirical validation pending.
