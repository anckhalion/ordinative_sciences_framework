# Pilot plan — version 0.1

Separate states: technical verification; diagnostic assessment; professional review; fitness for a specific use, where applicable. No state transfers automatically from audit to investigation.

## Preparation

Before any access to real data, establish authorisations, a suitable environment, data minimisation, roles and retention. The reviewer prepares the expected reference separately: inventory, verifiable facts, errors and decoys, expected anomalies, alternative explanations and adjudication criteria. The reference stays out of the analyst's package. Freeze frameworks, prompts, sources and the first output. Record the extraction/OCR tools and versions actually used.

## Minimum cases to cover (a coverage list, not a statistical validation threshold)

1. Harmless documentary duplicates, with no double payment.
2. Superseded versions and apparently irrelevant documents that become relevant once linked.
3. Gaps and illegible material: the system declares limited coverage without inventing content or concealment.
4. A controlled irregularity, with corroboration and at least one innocent explanation to verify.
5. A personal relationship or a coincidence of names that proves neither a conflict nor a responsibility.
6. An investigative case with a plausible adverse reconstruction refuted by evidence favourable to the defence.
7. An unresolved investigative case: the correct result keeps the alternatives open and issues no verdict.

## Measurement

For each expected finding: found, partial, missed, justified abstention. For additional findings: correct, false positive, unresolved. Precision and recall are computed only on adjudicated cases, with explicit denominators and exclusions. Errors are separated by type: acquisition/OCR, citation, linkage, diagnosis, confidence, legal interpretation. Premature exclusions, dependence between sources and unprocessed contrary elements are assessed as well.

The validator's synthetic tests populate none of these metrics. Initial diagnostic assessments remain exploratory. After corrections, use cases that were not employed to design the protocol. A version promotion requires a reasoned decision — a minimum number of tests or an uncalibrated score is insufficient on its own.

## Professional criminal-law review still required

The competent reviewer checks: the relevant legal system and time frame; roles and mandates; the rules applicable to documentation, statements and acquisitions; secrecy and confidentiality; personal and judicial data; conflicts; AI vendors and tools; archiving; procedural guarantees and admissibility. The list is open. Resolved and open questions are recorded without attributing powers or certifications to the software.
